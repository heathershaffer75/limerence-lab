💘 Limerence Lab
Interactive Recovery Neuroscience

Limerence Lab is an interactive psychoeducation tool designed to help patients understand why romantic obsession can feel emotionally overwhelming, addictive, euphoric, painful, and difficult to let go of.

This project blends:

Recovery neuroscience
Attachment theory
Trauma-informed psychoeducation
Dopamine and reward-system education
Emotional regulation concepts
Relationship pattern awareness
Fantasy vs reality differentiation
Practical recovery tools

The goal is to help patients move from:

“Why can’t I stop thinking about this person?”

to:

“My brain and nervous system entered a powerful emotional reward loop.”

🌱 Core Therapeutic Message

“Intensity is not always intimacy.”

And:

“Limerence often feeds on uncertainty, fantasy, longing, dopamine, and unmet emotional needs.”

This project helps patients understand:

Why obsession can feel physically addictive
Why uncertainty increases emotional intensity
Why intermittent attention fuels obsession
Why fantasy becomes emotionally rewarding
Why rejection can feel physically painful
Why the brain keeps “checking”
Why longing and attachment become emotionally fused
How recovery tools can interrupt the loop
